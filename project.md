# Project Brief: Original Generative AI Installation Inspired by *Unsupervised*

## Overview

Create an original large-scale generative art installation inspired by the experiential qualities of **Refik Anadol's *Unsupervised* (2022–2023), originally exhibited at the Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)**.

The objective is not to recreate or imitate *Unsupervised*, but to use it as a creative reference for the overall experience: an immersive digital artwork that appears to be continuously evolving, responding, and discovering new forms over time.

The finished installation should establish its own artistic identity while evoking the same sense of wonder, scale, fluidity, and perpetual transformation.

---

## Creative Vision

The installation should feel less like a video and more like a living digital organism.

Visuals should emerge organically, continuously transforming without obvious beginnings, endings, or repeated sequences. Motion should be smooth and deliberate, encouraging prolonged observation and rewarding viewers who spend time with the work.

Natural phenomena may serve as inspiration, including flowing water, ocean currents, clouds, smoke, ink dispersing through liquid, shifting landscapes, crystalline growth, weather systems, or other emergent processes. These references are intended to inspire an aesthetic direction rather than define specific visual outcomes.

The experience should be immersive, contemplative, and unmistakably original.

---

## Design Principles

The installation should strive to achieve the following qualities:

* Continuous evolution without visible looping
* Organic, fluid motion
* Monumental scale suitable for large-format displays
* Rich color, light, and depth
* A sense of internal intelligence or autonomous behavior
* Gradual emergence and transformation of visual structures
* Cohesive artistic identity that remains engaging over extended viewing

---

## Technical Considerations

There is no prescribed implementation for this project. Teams are encouraged to explore whichever creative and technical approaches best realize the artistic vision.

Possible areas of exploration include, but are not limited to:

* Real-time simulation
* Procedural generation
* GPU-accelerated rendering
* Fluid dynamics
* Particle systems
* Noise fields and vector fields
* Reaction-diffusion models
* Cellular automata
* Signed distance fields
* Volumetric rendering
* Generative shaders
* Physics-based animation
* Neural rendering
* Diffusion or latent-space exploration
* Machine learning–guided visual evolution
* Data-driven generative systems
* Environmental or audience-responsive behaviors
* Hybrid procedural and AI workflows

These examples are intended as sources of inspiration rather than technical requirements. Innovative techniques, emerging research, and experimental workflows are encouraged wherever they support the creative objectives.

---

## Environmental Interaction

The installation may incorporate external inputs that subtly influence its long-term evolution.

Potential inputs could include:

* Visitor movement
* Audience density
* Ambient sound
* Time of day
* Weather conditions
* Seasonal changes
* Live data feeds
* Other contextual information

Interactions should influence the artwork gradually rather than producing immediate or overt reactive effects, preserving the impression of a continuously evolving system.

---

## Performance Goals

The installation should be capable of sustained, uninterrupted operation in a public exhibition environment.

Desired characteristics include:

* High-resolution output suitable for museum-scale projection or LED walls
* Smooth real-time rendering
* Stable long-duration execution
* No discernible animation loops
* Graceful recovery following interruptions or restarts
* Consistent visual quality across extended viewing sessions

---

## Success Criteria

Success will be measured by the overall experience rather than the implementation.

A viewer should perceive the installation as an autonomous, living artwork that is constantly evolving. Every moment should feel unique while remaining visually coherent, inviting repeated observation and revealing new forms over time.

The finished work should stand on its own as an original piece of generative digital art, drawing inspiration from the experiential qualities of *Unsupervised* while expressing its own distinct artistic vision, technical approach, and visual language.
